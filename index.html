The method

Strength

Heavy, precise, clustered.

Cluster sets let you move heavy loads with quality on every rep — 2-2-1 and 3-2-1 schemes at 80–90% 1RM, with 15–20 sec between mini-sets.

  • Front Squat — Cluster 2-2-1
  • Barbell Bench Press — Cluster 3-2-1
  • Weighted Pull-Up

Hypertrophy

Hard sets, smart volume.

Supersets and accessory work at RPE 8–8.5 to build muscle without wasting time — with a no-machine alternative for every cable or machine exercise.

  • DB Incline Bench + Lat Pulldown
  • RDL + Leg Extension
  • Arms & abs every session

Explosiveness

Fast and powerful.

Plyometrics, speed work and conditioning so the strength you build turns into power you can use — focused on landing quality and coordination.

  • Drop Jump → Barrier Jump
  • Bench Press — Drop & Catch
  • Assault Bike sprints

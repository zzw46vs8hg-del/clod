About Lachie

Lachlan
McPherson

Athletic Performance Coach · Australian Men’s Bobsleigh Brakeman

  • B. Exercise & Sports Science
  • USAW-2
  • USATF-2

Lachlan has dedicated his career to bridging the gap between sports science and practical elite performance. Specialising in sprinting mechanics, Olympic weightlifting integration and plyometric force transfer, he has spent the past decade pursuing practical training knowledge, applying what works with high-performance athletes across multiple nations and disciplines.

He hasn’t just studied the field; he’s lived it. Lachlan competed in track and field as a decathlete, and has been a competitive weightlifter while competing for the Australian men’s national bobsleigh team.
